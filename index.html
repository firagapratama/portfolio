Delivery & Quality Lifecycle

How I orchestrate cross-functional teams and external client milestones from discovery to release.

6 PHASES
01 / SCOPE & REQUIREMENTS

SA & Client Alignment

Partner with external clients and System Analysts (SA) to translate business objectives into unambiguous technical specifications, edge case mappings, and milestone timelines.

02 / USABILITY & FEASIBILITY

UI/UX Design Review

Collaborate with UI/UX designers to evaluate user flows, interaction edge cases, and visual specifications against technical feasibility before engineering starts.

03 / SPRINT & DEVELOPMENT

Programmer (Dev) Execution

Guide Programmers (PG) through sprint execution with testable acceptance criteria, unblock architectural hurdles, and maintain realistic delivery velocity.

04 / QUALITY VERIFICATION

Multi-Layer QA Automation

Direct QA engineers across multi-layer test suites: Playwright E2E suites for user journeys, Postman API contract checks, and k6 performance baselines.

05 / RELEASE GOVERNANCE

Data-Driven Quality Gates

Make objective, data-backed go/no-go decisions based on enforced exit criteria: 100% smoke pass, ≥98% API, ≥95% UI, p95 under SLA, and zero critical bugs.

06 / CLIENT ACCEPTANCE & RELEASE

UAT & Production Sign-Off

Lead User Acceptance Testing (UAT) with external stakeholders, secure formal sign-off, and oversee smooth production deployments with zero guesswork.
