# Design Direction

## Identity
Portfolio for Firaga Pratama: Project Manager & Quality Assurance Lead. A technical delivery lead who bridges business requirements, client expectations, cross-functional orchestration (SA, UI/UX, Dev, QA), and strict go/no-go quality gates. The interactive CI pipeline simulator serves as the proof of release governance in practice.

## Audience
LinkedIn network, enterprise clients, business stakeholders, engineering leads, and technical hiring executives evaluating delivery leadership and quality engineering candidates.

## Palette
- **Base dark**: zinc-950 (#09090b)
- **Base light**: slate-50 (#f8fafc)
- **Primary text dark**: zinc-100 (#f4f4f5)
- **Muted text dark**: zinc-400 (#a1a1aa), minimum for any readable text
- **Functional only**: emerald for pass states, rose for fail states, sky for running states, amber for queued states. These colors appear exclusively inside the pipeline simulator and status badges. They do not decorate static content.
- **No decorative color**: section headers, badges, and timeline elements use neutral zinc tones.

## Typography
System font stack (ui-sans-serif, system-ui). Reason: invisible typeface that lets the content speak. This is a technical portfolio, not a brand showcase. The font should not draw attention.

## Mood
Precise, functional, restrained. Like a well-written test report: everything present has a reason, nothing decorative.

## Dials
Dial: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

- **ENERGY 1**: calm, professional. No hero animations, no glow, no gradient.
- **RHYTHM 2**: sections share outer containment style but vary internally. The pipeline section is the focal point (interactive, complex). Process and skills sections are clean structured layouts. Projects and Evidence Vault use their own specialized grids.
- **MOTION 1**: hover states only. No scroll-reveal, no parallax, no entrance animations. The only animation is the functional pulse on pipeline running states.

## Identity Motif
The pipeline. The interactive CI simulator is the one element that makes this portfolio recognizable. Everything else defers to it.
